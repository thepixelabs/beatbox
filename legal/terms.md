Hey there! Beatbox is a free little tool that turns WAV, AIFF and OGG files
into MP3s without uploading them. By using the site at `[SITE URL]`, you agree
to these simple rules.

## 1. How it works

Beatbox converts your files entirely in your own browser. Your files are never
uploaded. Because the work happens on your device, speed and which files
succeed depend on your browser and computer. We've tested it in Chrome.

## 2. No promises

Beatbox is free and provided "as is". We can't promise it'll work on every
device, browser or file. MP3 is a lossy format: it makes files smaller by
throwing away audio data, so the result is never identical to your original.
Some things are dropped on purpose or not checked, such as AIFF tags and
artwork.

## 3. Keep your originals

We never touch your files, so we can't recover them. To the extent the law
allows, we're not responsible if a conversion fails, a file comes out wrong,
or you don't like the result. Keep your original WAV or AIFF as your master.
